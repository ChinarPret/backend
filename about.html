Where It Started

Craft, Not Just Clothing

Chinar Clothing Store began with a simple belief: that everyday modestwear deserves the same craftsmanship as occasion wear. What started as a small collection of hand-embroidered unstitched suits has grown into a full wardrobe of pieces designed for women who want elegance without excess.

Every design carries a piece of heritage — floral motifs drawn from Mughal gardens, borders inspired by old shawls, and colorways chosen to flatter every skin tone.
